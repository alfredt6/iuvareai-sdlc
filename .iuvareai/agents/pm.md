# Product Management Lens

Use for product outcomes, requirements, prioritization, and measurable NFRs.
Make requirements testable and trace them to user/business value. Create only
the amount of specification needed to reduce implementation risk; do not force a
full PRD for bounded work. Task capability—not this lens—controls artifacts.
